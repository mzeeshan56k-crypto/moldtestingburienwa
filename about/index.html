How this service works

Mold Testing Burien WA is a referral and marketing service. We are not a mold inspection, testing or remediation company, and we don't send anyone to your home ourselves. Your choices

What providers are responsible for

Each provider is an independent business. They set their own prices, schedules and methods, and they are responsible for the quality of their work. Any licenses, certifications, insurance or guarantees belong to the provider and should be confirmed with them directly.

We encourage every caller to ask questions before booking: what training the inspector has, which lab analyzes samples, what the written report includes, how pricing works, and whether the company also performs remediation.

Our approach

What we believe about mold testing

These principles guide the information on this site.

Moisture comes first

Mold is a symptom. Every useful test should also look for the water that feeds it, or the problem comes back.

Independent checks matter

Testing by someone with no stake in the cleanup gives you results you can trust and share with insurers or buyers.

Plain answers

Lab reports can be confusing. We explain methods and terms so you can have a better conversation with your provider.

Burien's wet season, shoreline air and older housing shape mold risk. Our guides reflect those conditions honestly.

Who calls us

Who uses this service

People reach out at different moments, and the right next step depends on the situation.

Homeowners usually call after noticing a smell, a stain or a leak, or when someone in the family starts feeling worse at home. They want to know whether there's a real problem and where it's coming from. A full inspection is the usual starting point.

Buyers and sellers want documentation. Buyers ask for attic and crawl space checks before closing, and sellers sometimes test before listing so they can address issues on their own schedule. Waterfront and hillside homes are frequent cases.

Renters and landlords need a neutral record. A report with photos and readings helps both sides agree on what's happening and what needs fixing, without guesswork.

People finishing a cleanup want proof that it worked. Independent clearance testing gives them confidence before walls are closed or a family moves back in.

How to get the most from your call

Have the basics ready: where you've noticed the problem, how long it has been going on, and any repairs already made. Then ask the provider plainly what they recommend and why. A good inspector will explain whether testing is likely to change what you do next, and will tell you when a simple fix is enough.
